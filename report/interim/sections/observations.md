Preliminary results (untuned, single seed 42): on the test split the 1D-CNN (macro-F1 0.941) and the MLP on engineered features (0.939) lead, ahead of the BiLSTM (0.915), Transformer (0.902) and GRU (0.895). Validation scores are closer together (0.951 to 0.970) and rank the models differently. Every model scores lower on test than on validation, with macro-F1 dropping by 0.012 for the MLP up to 0.068 for the GRU, so validation alone overstates generalisation to new subjects, most for the recurrent and Transformer models. SITTING/STANDING confusion is the largest single error for every model, 48% to 86% of its test errors. The GRU and Transformer also confuse the three walking activities far more often than the CNN.
